# Needledrop

A spinning vinyl, a real music player and a volume mixer for every sound in Minecraft. Client side, Fabric, 26.3.

**Disc overlay.** Cover art, title, artist and controls in a corner of the pause menu, title screen or HUD. Move it, resize it, recolor it, or hide it.

**Music player.** Your own mp3, ogg and wav files plus Minecraft's songs and discs. Several folders, search, queue, shuffle, loop, per-song removal.

**Media apps.** Show and control YouTube Music, Spotify and other players through your system's media session (Windows works out of the box, Linux needs playerctl, macOS needs nowplaying-cli).

**Sound mixer.** One slider for every sound: animals, hostile mobs, vehicles, blocks, items, music, weather, interface and more.

Requires Fabric API. Mod Menu optional.
