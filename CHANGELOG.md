# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Smooth vinyl disc drawn as one anti-aliased texture (the rectangle disc stays as a fallback).
- Cleaner interface: rounded panels, switches, softer colors.
- Remove any single song from the list, including Minecraft's own songs and songs from folders. Files are never deleted.
- Minecraft songs can be switched off from the Music tab. Reset all and Restore bring them back.
- Hide the disc on the pause menu or the title screen from its right-click menu.
- Fixed mouse buttons on Minecraft 26.3 (SDL numbering).
- Lower overhead: cached lists, cached sound lookups, no work in the sound hook when nothing is adjusted.
- New name and icon.

## 1.0.x
- First versions: disc overlay, local player, system media sync, sound mixer.
